João leads the JUST Lab's work on microalgal platforms for genetic engineering,
recombinant-protein production, and sustainable bioprocesses. He holds a FAPESP
Young Investigator award and coordinates the MICROPLAT project developing a
microalgal biopharmaceutical production platform.
