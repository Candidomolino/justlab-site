Biopharmaceuticals are a large and growing cost for public health systems, and Brazil depends on foreign technology to produce them — a vulnerability exposed by supply disruptions during the COVID-19 pandemic. Gaucher disease, treated by lifelong enzyme replacement, is a clear case: the Ministry of Health spends about US$140 million a year on the reference treatment. MICROPLAT proposes a national platform to produce Creglicerase, a human β-glucocerebrosidase (hGCase) expressed in *Chlamydomonas reinhardtii*. Building on the group's expression and secretion tools and a screening method that can process more than 500,000 transformants, it aims to be the first platform to secrete active hGCase with a therapeutically suitable glycosylation pattern, without special culture interventions. A new fusion strategy for smart release of cathepsin blockers may extend the drug's half-life. The project also trains specialists in biopharmaceutical production.
